The Short Version

One platform, four apps, your brand on all of them.

Verlilian Dispatch pairs a rider-facing booking app with a driver app, a management dashboard for your team, and a live dispatcher view for coordinating trips as they happen. Everything ships under your name — ours doesn't appear anywhere the public can see.

FAQ

How long does launch actually take?

Once branding assets and your pricing model are confirmed, we typically move from discovery call to a live testable build within one to two weeks, depending on app store review times.

Yes. Source code is handed over as part of the package — there's no ongoing licence fee to keep running what you paid for.
