Description of Principal Business

Every close corporation registered in Namibia has to state its principal business on a single line of a government form. Ours reads: refer to the attached annexure.

What follows is that annexure, in full: every trade Verlilian Investment CC is registered to carry out, grouped by discipline so a contractor, procurement officer, or client knows exactly what we can do.

Working With Verlilian

What one registration gets you

Single point of registration

CC/2022/01587 covers every trade in the manifest above. One registration number, one accounting officer, one company to vet — instead of chasing a different supplier for each part of a complex project.

Port-city logistics

The registered office sits at Walvis Bay, Namibia's principal harbour. Import, export, and transport work starts at the quay, not three towns inland.

Built to scale sideways

A Namibian close corporation is structured for exactly this: full legal standing, clear liability, and the room to take on whichever registered activity a contract actually calls for at any moment.
